Our Identity

Built on Trust,
Driven by Science

Dr Bashir Sons Traders is a Pakistani veterinary pharma trading house specializing in poultry feed medicines, dairy health products, antibiotics, nutritional supplements, and feed additives. With roots in Okara, Punjab, we serve farmers, veterinarians, distributors, and institutional buyers with consistent quality and practical guidance.

Our export network connects international buyers with Pakistan-based pharmaceutical and nutritional solutions, bridging the gap between regional product sources and professional global trade requirements.

Our Mission
Elevate Animal Well-being
To enhance the health and productivity of animals through effective healthcare solutions while empowering livestock owners and caretakers with knowledge, products, and support.
Our Vision
A Trusted Global Name
To be a trusted name in animal healthcare trading, recognized for excellence, ethics, compassion, and support for sustainable livestock practices.
