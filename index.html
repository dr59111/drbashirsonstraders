Who We Are

A Focused Distribution Channel for Pakistan's Agricultural Sector

Our Coverage

Operating in Pakistan's Agricultural Heartland

Punjab is Pakistan's most important agricultural and poultry-producing province. Our primary distribution network covers the major poultry and livestock belts of central and southern Punjab.

Pakistan produces over 1.5 billion broiler birds annually. Punjab alone accounts for the majority of this production. Our network gives international manufacturers immediate access to this critical market without the need for local entity setup.
